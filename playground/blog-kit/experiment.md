# Theme override and simulation

This lesson uses a teal accent and a wide reading column. Both come from its entry in `content.lessons` in `mrmd.config.json`.

## Explore the scene

On a desktop-width screen, expand the lab and move the **Position** slider from 50% to 75%. The marker moves along the line. Its starting value, slider range, and 280 px preview height come from `marker.config.json`.
