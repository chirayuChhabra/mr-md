# Welcome to Blog Kit

This page uses the chapter defaults: a blue accent and a narrow reading column. The order in `content.lessons` sets the sidebar and Next Lesson links.

## Change the chapter

Edit `theme.settings.accent` in `mrmd.config.json` and save to change the color on this page. The preview rebuilds automatically.

## Try the testing replacement

Choose an answer. This chapter selects `testing.js` in the `testing` section of `mrmd.config.json`. The replacement supplies its own HTML, styles, and answer handling.

![Check your understanding](./check.json)

## Lesson overrides

Follow **Next Lesson** to see a wider page with a teal accent. Both changes are set on that lesson's entry in the chapter config.
