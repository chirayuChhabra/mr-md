# Orbital Dynamics & Conserved Quantities

In celestial mechanics, the motion of an orbiting body around a primary gravitational mass is governed by Newton's law of universal gravitation and Kepler's laws of planetary motion.

Two fundamental physical quantities remain conserved throughout any closed two-body orbit: **specific orbital energy** ($\varepsilon$) and **specific angular momentum** ($h$):

$$\varepsilon = \frac{v^2}{2} - \frac{\mu}{r} = -\frac{\mu}{2a}$$

$$h = \|\vec{r} \times \vec{v}\| = r v_\perp = \sqrt{\mu a (1 - e^2)}$$
